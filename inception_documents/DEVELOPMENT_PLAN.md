# Development Plan

### Risk Driven Iteration Strategy
Our Development plan will prioritize high risk exposure technical risks in iteration 1 to ensure they do not occur.

| Iteration | Due Date | Milestone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 09/20/26 | Working app on both platforms and node edge graph architecture setup.
| 2 | 10/11/26 | Indoor and outdoor navigation engine implemented. Nedderman Hall fully mapped out as a proof of concept.
| 3 | 11/01/26 | Remainder of campus mapped out and parking helper integrated. MyMav schedule parser included.
| 4 | 11/15/26 | Any changes required for final delivery. Aesthetics improvement and minor changes.

---

## Detailed Iteration Breakdown

### Iteration 1:
- Setup React Native or other framework to unify iOS and Android development
- Design coordinate node edge data interfaces
- Start early work on mapping rooms of Nedderman Hall

### Iteration 2:
- Implement Dijkstra or similar path finding algorithm for pathfinding support
- Fully map out Nedderman Hall and surrounding outdoor locations to demo the product
- Ensure text navigation instructions are accurate and working

### Iteration 3:
- Fully map out campus and relevant surrounding apartments and parking lots
- Integrate indoor routing helper
- Integrate MyMav schedule parser
- Integrate parking helper based on provided classes
- Gather the interviewed users for a prototype test

### Iteration 4:
- Finalize all features
- Test on both iOS and Android platforms for any compatibility issues
- Finalize demo and presentation
